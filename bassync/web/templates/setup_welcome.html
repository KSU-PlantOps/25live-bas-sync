Welcome

This guide gets the sync running: it connects to 25Live, finds your rooms there, and points each building at the BAS schedule its bookings should drive. It takes about fifteen minutes, and you can leave and come back — every step saves as you go, and each one is also on the other pages.

Nothing is written to your BAS until you're done. The schedule stays off until the last step, and buildings found in 25Live wait on a preview system, which writes nothing, until you give each one its schedule.

You'll need

  • A local 25Live account (not single sign-on) with Series25 WebServices turned on and read access to the events and locations to schedule.
  • For BACnet: this host on the controls network, and a BACnet device ID for the sync that nothing else on campus uses. Your controls team will know both.
  • For each building, the schedule the sync should own — a BACnet schedule set aside for bookings. You can stop before this step and come back when you have them.
